About JobNest

A smarter career platform for job seekers

JobNest is built to help students, graduates, and professionals discover the right jobs faster. The platform makes it easy to search by role, category, skill, location, and salary while keeping the experience clean, modern, and beginner-friendly.

Our aim is to simplify hiring and job discovery through a user-first design, modern front-end experience, and features such as saved jobs, applications tracking, and personalized profile management.
